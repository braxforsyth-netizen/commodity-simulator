How the desk works

  1. Clients ask for a two-way price in Brent futures. You don't know if they're buying or selling. Set your width and skew, then send. Tight quotes win more trades. Wide quotes earn more per trade.
  2. Know who you're pricing. Corporates hedge real exposure and don't know where price is going. Hedge funds often do, and if they trade on a tight price you usually lose. The trading house is somewhere in between.
  3. Every fill leaves you with risk. Hedge on screen (you pay the market spread), or skew your next quotes so clients take the position off you for free.
  4. Headlines move the fair value. The traded price catches up over a few ticks. Read, decide bullish or bearish, act. Rumours move it less, and some get denied.
  5. Limits are real. Every tick over your position limit is fined. At 1.5× the risk manager cuts you at a bad price. Losing past the desk's stop-loss ends your day.
